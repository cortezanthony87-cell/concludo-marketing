Where Concludo is today

The Concludo Workspace is a pre-release preview. You can create an account and try it for 14 days. It is still being built, features are incomplete, and it is not yet offered for paid or customer use. This policy describes what we do today. Before we open the Workspace for real customer use, we will replace this policy with one that covers customer transcripts, and we will say so on this site.

What we collect today

  • Messages you send us. If you email hello@concludo.au, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and what you write. We use it to reply.
  • Website visits. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, it may log your IP address and basic request details such as the page requested and the time.
  • Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google. This sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.
  • Cookies and tracking. At the date above, this website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy first.
  • Calculator. The Meeting Cost Calculator runs in your browser. We do not receive the figures you enter.
  • Preview accounts. If you create an account to try the pre-release Workspace, we hold the email address you sign in with, your sign-in details (managed by our authentication provider) and whatever you choose to enter. Please use sample information only. Do not enter real client transcripts, recordings or personal information about other people while the Workspace is a preview. We may delete preview accounts and their content at any time, and we will delete yours if you ask.

How we use it

To reply to you, to run and secure this website, and to improve our products. We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use these providers: GitHub (website hosting), Google (email and fonts) and Supabase (sign-in and storage for the pre-release Workspace). Some of them may store or process information outside Australia.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to deal with it, and then delete it unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask us for the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@concludo.au. We will reply within 30 days.

Complaints

Email hello@concludo.au with “Privacy complaint” in the subject line. We will acknowledge it within 5 business days and respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.

General information only, not legal advice.
